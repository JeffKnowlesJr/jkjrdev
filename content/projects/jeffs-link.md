jeffs.link is a public Obsidian vault — a working second brain made visible. Notes on daily logging, developer references, and knowledge organization are published here so others can explore the structure and adapt it for their own use.

The vault is built on Obsidian Publish and served through Cloudflare. It's both a personal tool and a demonstration of what a connected note-taking system looks like in practice.

## What's inside

- Daily notes templates and organization guides
- Developer and tool references
- Graph view showing how notes connect across topics

## Why it's public

Obsidian changed how I capture and find information. Publishing the vault makes the system tangible — you can see real notes, real structure, and real links rather than a marketing demo.
