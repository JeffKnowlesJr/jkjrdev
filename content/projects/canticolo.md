A chord chart app for beginner musicians learning guitar, piano, recorder, mandolin, or harmonica.

## Features

- ChordPro editor with chord suggestions, auto-save, and draft recovery
- Chord voicing diagrams for five instruments
- Instrument reference views with audio playback
- Perform mode with auto-scroll, transposition, and capo
- Theory tools: Circle of Fifths, Chord Explorer, scale and progression sandbox
- Cloud sync, public song sharing, and export to .txt or .chopro
