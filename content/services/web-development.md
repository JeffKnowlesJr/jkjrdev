## What I Build

I've worked across the full range of web technologies — from simple business sites to production web applications. I've seen what lasts and what doesn't. That perspective is what I bring to every project.

My work spans:

**Business websites** — professional sites for consultants, professional services firms, contractors, and local businesses that need to show up in search, convert visitors, and represent the business accurately.

**Web applications** — custom software built for the web: client portals, internal tools, workflow automation, SaaS products. If a spreadsheet or off-the-shelf tool is no longer cutting it, there's likely a web application that solves your problem better.

**Platform migrations** — moving sites off legacy platforms that have become a liability. WordPress that breaks with every update. Concrete5 sites that nobody can edit. Squarespace sites that have hit the platform's ceiling. I migrate to whatever makes sense: Webflow for marketing sites, Next.js for applications.

**Site redesigns** — modernizing the look, structure, and performance of a site that's embarrassing to hand out a business card with.

## How I Work

I work directly with clients — there's no account manager between you and the person actually building your site. You'll know what's being built, why, and what it costs before any work begins.

My typical process:

1. **Initial conversation** — you tell me what you have, what you need, and what's not working. I ask questions. No sales pitch.
2. **Proposal** — a clear document describing what I'll build, the technology I'll use, the timeline, and the cost. Fixed-price for defined-scope work; hourly for open-ended engagements.
3. **Build** — regular check-ins and previews so there are no surprises at the end.
4. **Launch** — I handle deployment, DNS, performance verification, and analytics setup.
5. **After launch** — I'm available for ongoing support, additions, and changes.

## The Technology I Use

I match the technology to the project:

- **Webflow** — for marketing sites, portfolios, and informational sites where the client needs editorial control without touching code
- **Next.js + React** — for web applications, sites with complex data requirements, and projects where performance and SEO are paramount
- **WordPress** — for clients who need a mature CMS ecosystem or are already running WordPress and need development work done within it

All my projects include: Google Analytics 4, structured data (schema.org), sitemap and robots configuration, and mobile-responsive design.

## Who I Work With

I work best with:

- **Small businesses** in Northern Virginia, the DC metro area, and the surrounding region that need a real developer — not a template and a drag-and-drop builder
- **Startups** at the early stage that need a marketing site or MVP built quickly by someone who understands the technical decisions that will matter later
- **Professional services firms** — lawyers, architects, consultants, healthcare practices — that need a site that builds trust with prospective clients
- **Organizations** that have outgrown their current site or platform

I don't work well with clients who want the absolute cheapest option, who need to hire a large team, or who need enterprise-scale managed services. If that's what you need, I'll tell you.

## Based in Northern Virginia

I'm based in Northern Virginia and work primarily with clients in the DC metro area — Fairfax, Arlington, Reston, McLean, Alexandria, and Washington DC itself. I'm available for in-person meetings when that's useful.

That said, most of my work is remote and I have clients outside the region. Geography doesn't limit what we can build together.
